## TL;DR

No ads, analytics, or tracking. This site is served through Cloudflare, which provides its DNS and CDN.

## Details

**Myself**: I do not run advertising, analytics, tracking, or a newsletter. I do not set cookies or keep visitor logs myself.

**Cloudflare**: Cloudflare provides DNS and CDN services for this site. It processes requests on its network before they reach the site, which may include IP addresses, traffic-routing data, system configuration information, and other request data. Cloudflare acts as my service provider for this processing. Its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) explains its handling in more detail.

**Apple Music**: The homepage displays a widget showing my recently played Apple Music songs. This request is about my account only; no visitor account, listening history, or other personal data is sent to Apple or accessed by this site. Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-music/) explains Apple's handling in more detail.

## Why

This limited technical processing is necessary for my legitimate interest in keeping the site reliable and secure, including preventing abuse. I do not use it to identify you, build a profile, or market to you.
