AI is a tool. I see value in using it to augment human capability, not to replace it.

My goal when using AI is to **work smarter, not work less**. I use it to explore ideas, automate repetitive work, learn, prototype, and generally extend what I can do on my own.

That doesn't mean I blindly trust it.

## Human judgment

AI can make mistakes, confidently invent things, and produce output that looks convincing without actually being correct. Because of that, **AI doesn't get the final say**.

Major decisions are ultimately made by me. I remain responsible for what I publish, build, and endorse, regardless of whether AI was involved.

## Review and understanding

I'll **never publish or use AI-generated output without understanding, reviewing and auditing it first**.

If AI writes code, I'll understand what the code does before shipping it. If it provides information, I'll verify it when accuracy matters. If it makes a recommendation, I'll consider whether the reasoning actually holds up.

Using AI doesn't remove the responsibility to think.

## Transparency

If AI had a meaningful hand in creating something, **I'll say so**.

I don't think AI involvement is inherently something to hide, and I would rather people know how something was made than leave them to guess.

I also appreciate the same transparency from other people. If something was substantially created or assisted by AI, I'd rather be told than have it presented as entirely human-made.
